          The pipeline

          Stage timings appear on the diagram after an analysis on the Try it tab.

          Why two models?

          An emotion lives in two places: how something is said and what is said. "Great, just great" is joy or anger depending on the tone, while a flat voice saying "my dog died" is still sad.

          👂 The sound model listens to the voice: it reads the Whisper encoder's picture of the audio, which carries pitch, energy and rhythm. 💬 The text model reads the words that Whisper transcribed from the same pass.

          Each is right on different clips.

          Before mixing them, each model's confidence is calibrated (temperature scaling), so that "60% sure" means right about 60% of the time. Then the two are combined with a weight chosen on held-out data.

          When the two disagree with confidence, the app says so instead of hiding it. That is where sarcasm and masked feelings tend to show up.
